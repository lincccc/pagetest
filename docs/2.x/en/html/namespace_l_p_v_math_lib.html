Detailed Description

LPV Math& Data analysis Library, provide mathematics and data analysis functionalities.

This library provides miscellaneous functions for data calculation, filtering, aggregation and display. To include the definitions of the library's classes, use the following directive:

Example Code

C++
#include "LPVMath.h"
C#
using LPVMathLib;
LPVMathLib

◆ LPVDistanceType

enum LPVDistanceType

This enumeration represents the distance metric between two numeric vectors. Smaller value usually means the vectors are more similar.

In the equations below, A and B are the two vectors to compare.

Enumerator
LPVDistanceL1 

L1 distance, aka. Manhattan distance: \( \sum_{i}{\left|a_i - b_i\right|} \)

LPVDistanceL2 

L2 distance, aka. Euclidean distance: \( \sqrt{\sum_{i}{\left(a_i - b_i\right)^2}} \)

LPVDistanceLInf 

L-infinity distance, aka. Chebyshev distance: \( \max_{i}{\left|a_i - b_i\right|} \)

LPVDistanceLp 

Minkowski distance with the given exponent \( p \ge 1 \) , p is the Minkowski exponent: \( \left(\sum_{i}{\left|a_i - b_i\right|^p}\right)^{1/p} \)

LPVDistanceCosine 

Cosine distance: \( 1 - \frac{A \cdot B}{\left\|A\right\| \left\|B\right\|} \)

LPVDistanceCorrelation 

Correlation distance, one minus the Pearson correlation coefficient: \( 1 - \rho\left(A, B\right) \)

LPVDistanceCanberra 

Canberra distance: \( \sum_{i}{\frac{\left|a_i - b_i\right|}{\left|a_i\right| + \left|b_i\right|}} \)

LPVDistanceBrayCurtis 

Bray-Curtis distance, commonly used for non-negative data: \( \frac{\sum_{i}{\left|a_i - b_i\right|}}{\sum_{i}{\left|a_i + b_i\right|}} \)

◆ LPVDistributionType

enum LPVDistributionType

This enumeration represents the metric between two histograms or probability distributions.

In the equations below, A and B are the two distributions to compare, ai and bi are the values of their ith bins.

Note
Hellinger, Bhattacharyya, KLDivergence and JensenShannon assume non-negative, normalized (probability) inputs, no automatic normalization is performed.
Enumerator
LPVDistributionIntersection 

Histogram intersection: \( \sum_{i}{\min\left(a_i, b_i\right)} \)

LPVDistributionChiSquare 

Pearson's chi-square statistic using the first distribution as denominator (asymmetric): \( \sum_{i}{\frac{\left(a_i - b_i\right)^2}{a_i}} \)

LPVDistributionChiSquareAlt 

Symmetric chi-square variant: \( 2\sum_{i}{\frac{\left(a_i - b_i\right)^2}{a_i + b_i}} \)

LPVDistributionHellinger 

Hellinger distance: \( \sqrt{1 - \sum_{i}{\sqrt{a_i b_i}}} \)

LPVDistributionCorrelation 

Pearson correlation coefficient between the bins: \( \rho\left(A, B\right) \)

LPVDistributionBhattacharyya 

Bhattacharyya distance: \( -\ln{\left(\sum_{i}{\sqrt{a_i b_i}}\right)} \)

LPVDistributionKLDivergence 

Kullback-Leibler divergence \( D_{KL}\left(A \| B\right) \) , the terms with non-positive ai or bi contribute 0: \( \sum_{i}{a_i \ln{\frac{a_i}{b_i}}} \)

LPVDistributionJensenShannon 

Jensen-Shannon divergence, symmetric and smoothed KL divergence: \( \frac{1}{2}D_{KL}\left(A \| M\right) + \frac{1}{2}D_{KL}\left(B \| M\right), M = \frac{A + B}{2} \)

LPVDistributionTotalVariation 

Total variation distance: \( \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i}{\left|a_i - b_i\right|} \)

LPVDistributionEMD 

Earth Mover's Distance, aka. the 1D Wasserstein-1 distance, it is the minimal work to transform one distribution into the other: \( \min_{\gamma \in \Pi\left(A, B\right)}{\sum_{i,j}{\gamma_{ij}\left|i - j\right|}} \)

◆ LPVSimilarityType

enum LPVSimilarityType

This enumeration represents the similarity or correlation metric between two numeric vectors. Larger value usually means the vectors are more similar.

In the equations below, A and B are the two vectors to compare.

Enumerator
LPVSimilarityCosine 

Cosine similarity: \( \frac{A \cdot B}{\left\|A\right\| \left\|B\right\|} \)

LPVSimilarityPearson 

Pearson correlation coefficient, the linear correlation after mean removal: \( \rho\left(A, B\right) \)

LPVSimilaritySpearman 

Spearman rank correlation coefficient, the Pearson correlation coefficient of the ranks: \( \rho\left(R\left(A\right), R\left(B\right)\right) \)

LPVSimilarityKendall 

Kendall rank correlation coefficient (tau-a), C and D are the counts of concordant and discordant pairs, n is the count of the values, the ties are not counted: \( \frac{C - D}{n\left(n - 1\right)/2} \)
